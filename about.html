About Royal Spark

Crafting Elegance Since 1998

Our Story

Royal Spark began in 1998 as a small jewellery workshop in Chennai with a dream - to create jewellery that tells a story.

Today we are proud to be one of South India's most trusted names for 916 Hallmark Gold, Diamond Jewellery, and Traditional Bridal Sets.

For 25+ years, we have combined traditional craftsmanship with modern designs. Every piece is made with love and purity.
